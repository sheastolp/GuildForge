# GuildForge Branding Guide

## Name
**GuildForge**

## Taglines (use all three)

1. **Primary / Hero**  
   Forge your stream. Command your chat.

2. **Secondary**  
   Tools for the guild. Power for the stream.

3. **Descriptive**  
   Chatbots, overlays & ambient tools — forged for streamers.

## Product Naming

Always prefer the full form when introducing a tool:

- GuildForge Guildscribe
- GuildForge Hunt & Hoard
- GuildForge Hushwave
- GuildForge Chat Overlay
- GuildForge Mic Status

Short forms are fine once the brand context is established.

## Voice & Tone
- Friendly and empowering
- Light fantasy flavor without being cringe
- Clear and practical (streamers care about results)
- Inclusive of all stream sizes and experience levels

## Visual Direction (for future logos / assets)
- Primary feel: modern forge + subtle fantasy (anvil, hammer, subtle runes, stream icons)
- Colors: deep charcoal / dark slate + warm ember orange + clean white/silver accents
- Avoid overly cartoon or overly corporate looks
