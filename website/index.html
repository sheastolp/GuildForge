The Tools

Guildscribe

DnD-themed Twitch chatbot. A powerful, fantasy-flavored alternative to StreamElements.

Hunt & Hoard

Idle, no-end RPG game running entirely through a Twitch chatbot. Viewers hunt, gather, and build their hoard.

Hushwave

Ambient music generator for Windows. Light, atmospheric soundscapes for your stream.

Chat Overlay

Clean, customizable OBS browser-source chat overlay. Ready to drop into your scenes.

Mic Status

Live microphone status overlay for OBS. Let your viewers know when you’re on mic.

Get Started

All tools are open-source. Clone the repositories, follow the setup guides inside each package, point your bots and OBS sources, and go live. Core features stay free. Future Pro / hosted options will unlock advanced features under one GuildForge account.
